## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive business analysis comparing **Swiggy** and **Zomato**, India’s two leading food delivery platforms. The analysis was performed using **SQL**, **Power BI**, and **data analytics techniques** to uncover insights related to:

* Revenue performance
* Profitability
* Market share
* Restaurant ratings
* Delivery efficiency
* Cuisine popularity
* Platform dominance
* Pricing category trends

The project combines **13 SQL analytical queries** with an interactive **Power BI dashboard** to transform raw restaurant data into actionable business intelligence.

# 🎯 Objectives of the Project

The main objective of this project is to analyze and compare restaurant performance across Swiggy and Zomato using business intelligence tools.

The analysis focuses on:

1. Understanding platform-wise revenue and profit trends
2. Identifying top-performing restaurant categories
3. Comparing market share across restaurant types
4. Evaluating operational efficiency through delivery times
5. Analyzing customer ratings and order behavior
6. Understanding pricing category performance
7. Discovering city-wise platform dominance

# 📊 Dashboard Components

## 1️⃣ Order Comparison — Swiggy vs Zomato

Compares monthly order trends across both platforms.

### Insights:

* Both platforms show competitive order volumes
* Seasonal spikes can be identified
* Zomato slightly leads in several months

---

## 2️⃣ Orders by Restaurant Type

Analyzes order distribution across restaurant categories.

### Insights:

* Cloud Kitchens dominate the market
* Fine Dine contributes lower volume but higher revenue per order
* QSR and Cafes maintain balanced performance

---

## 3️⃣ City-wise Rating Comparison

Compares customer ratings across cities.

### Insights:

* Mumbai and Bangalore have the highest customer engagement
* Ratings remain relatively stable across cities
* Average ratings range around 4.0

---

## 4️⃣ Revenue by City

Displays city-level revenue performance.

### Insights:

* Mumbai generates the highest revenue
* Bangalore and Delhi are major contributors
* Smaller cities show growth opportunities

---

## 5️⃣ Market Share Analysis

Shows overall market dominance between Swiggy and Zomato.

### Insights:

* Swiggy dominates Cloud Kitchens
* Zomato performs better in Fine Dine and Casual Dining
* Competition remains highly balanced overall

---

## 6️⃣ Delivery Time Comparison

Compares delivery efficiency across cities.

### Insights:

* Both platforms maintain nearly identical delivery times
* Average delivery duration is around 40 minutes
* Long-distance deliveries exceed 50 minutes

---

## 7️⃣ Monthly Order Trends

Tracks monthly order fluctuations.

### Insights:

* Seasonal demand spikes are visible
* March and October show strong order growth
* Platform trends remain closely competitive

---

## 8️⃣ Revenue by Price Category

Analyzes revenue generated from different pricing segments.

### Insights:

* Premium and Luxury categories generate maximum revenue
* Budget category contributes high volume but lower profit
* Luxury segment yields the highest average profit
